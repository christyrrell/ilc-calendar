How to Subscribe

Pick your calendar app. You only need to do this once.

iPhone · iPad · Mac

Apple Calendar

  1. Tap Subscribe to all events above.
  2. Confirm with Subscribe.
Android · Web

Google Calendar

  1. Tap Add to Google Calendar above and confirm with Add.
  2. If that doesn't work: on a computer, next to Other calendars, click + → From URL and paste this link:
/calendar.ics
Web or desktop

Outlook

  1. Choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  2. Paste this link:
/calendar.ics

Only Want Some Events?

Subscribe to a single category instead of everything.

    Combine categories with commas, e.g. ?cal=worship-services,holidays

    Events come from the church's public ChurchTrac calendar and update automatically.

    Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule (often every 12–24 hours); Apple and Outlook check more often.
